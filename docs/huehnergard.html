About

I am a researcher and writer interested in the ways ideas, institutions, and technologies shape one another.

My work sits at the intersection of theory and practice. I am particularly interested in careful scholarship, clear writing, and the development of useful frameworks for understanding complicated subjects.

This site is a small record of my work and interests. It is intentionally simple: a place for publications, notes, projects, and a few words about what I am currently thinking about.

Interests

My broader interests include the history of ideas, institutions, technology, methodology, and the relationship between theory and empirical evidence.

I am also interested in questions that resist neat disciplinary boundaries. Some of the most useful problems, in my experience, begin precisely where established categories become insufficient.
